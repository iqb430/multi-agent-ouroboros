# Shifted Debate Launch Copy

## 1. Medium Article
**The Reality Bypass: Why "AI Coding Bootcamps" Are Selling You a Mirage**

The tech industry is treating software development like a vending machine. Put a token in, press a button, and out pops a senior engineer. The internet is flooded with "AI Coding Bootcamps" promising to turn novices into mid-level developers in six weeks, entirely powered by large language models writing the code for you. It functions like a mechanical exoskeleton for a toddler. Yes, the machine can lift the heavy box, but the pilot still doesn't know how gravity works.

The organizations selling these bootcamps don't care about building competence. They care about scale and subscription revenue. It is the commodification of a skillset reduced to a series of API calls. The students aren't learning how to architect systems or debug complex logic. They are learning how to prompt a machine to string together boilerplate. This isn't education. It's dependency. The market is capitalizing on the desperation of a workforce terrified of obsolescence, selling them a bypass around the actual, grueling work of mastery. It’s an arrangement where the platform claims the victory while the student remains fundamentally helpless the moment the context window fails.

Fast-forward five years. The market is saturated with "prompt engineers" who cannot read a stack trace or understand the underlying logic of the systems they deploy. Security vulnerabilities multiply because the operators only know how to ask the AI to build the wall, not how to inspect the bricks. When a critical production error occurs, they are paralyzed, waiting for a model to fix a problem it created. The actual engineers—the ones who spent the time building the mental models the hard way—become the indispensable janitors of a collapsing digital infrastructure. There is no shortcut. You either build the mental models yourself, or you rent them until you break.
